2. GOOGLE AUTHENTICATION CONFIGURATION (SUPABASE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To enable "Continue with Google" for real customer accounts:

1. In Google Cloud Console (https://console.cloud.google.com/apis/credentials):
   - Create an OAuth 2.0 Client ID (Web Application).
   - Add Authorized redirect URI:
     https://wacjxpjeunelkormmxvu.supabase.co/auth/v1/callback

2. In Supabase Dashboard (https://supabase.com/dashboard/project/wacjxpjeunelkormmxvu):
   - Navigate to: Authentication -> Providers -> Google.
   - Toggle "Enable Google provider" ON.
   - Paste your Google "Client ID" and "Client Secret".
   - Click Save.

3. Enforcement & Account Security (Already Implemented in Code):
   - Only authentic Google accounts are accepted.
   - Fake or dummy email forms are disabled.
   - Customer orders are strictly isolated to their authenticated Google account.
